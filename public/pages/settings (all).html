Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using the portal.
How do I view my class schedule?
Go to Class Schedule from the menu bar. It shows your weekly timetable with subject times, days, rooms, and instructors.
How do I enroll in a course?
Go to Course Enrollment from the menu bar, select your subjects from the list of available courses, and confirm your enrollment. Prerequisites are checked automatically.
Where can I check my grades?
Go to Grades & Records from the menu bar to view your grades per subject and semester, along with your academic standing.
How can I check my attendance and assignments?
Go to Assignments & Attendance from the menu bar. It shows your submission status for assignments and your attendance logs per class.
How do I check my tuition assessment?
Go to Tuition Assessment from the menu bar to view your fee breakdown, balance, and payment history.
